What we do

The service

Batt insulation looks like the simplest trade on the job and is the one most often done badly. Compressed batt loses its rated value, gaps at the top plate undo the assembly, and a vapour barrier with unsealed penetrations is barely a barrier at all. Inspectors know it when they see it, and so do we.

Our crews cut batt to fit the cavity and friction-hold it in place, work it in properly around boxes, conduit and piping, then lap and seal the poly at every stud, plate and penetration. Acoustic assemblies in demising and corridor walls get the same treatment, because that is where the sound complaints come from.

Labour only: you order the batt and the poly to your schedule and to your specified R-value. We bring the crew, the knives and the staplers, and we work to whatever assembly is drawn.

Scope we cover

  • Wall cavity batt — thermal and acoustic
  • Ceiling and floor-assembly batt
  • Sound batt in demising and corridor walls
  • Poly vapour barrier, lapped and sealed
  • Acoustic sealant at plates and penetrations
  • Cavity fill around boxes, conduit and piping
  • Safing and stuffing at deck and slab edges
  • Rim joist and header insulation
  • Batt supports and mechanical fastening
  • Clean-down and offcut removal

What you actually get

Installation

Tight cavities, no compression

Batt cut to fit and friction-held, never jammed or stretched. Compressed batt does not deliver its rated value and an inspector will call it.

Vapour barrier

Poly that actually seals

Lapped at the studs, sealed at every penetration and taped at the joints. A barrier is only as good as its worst detail, so all of them get treated the same.

Acoustics

Inspection
